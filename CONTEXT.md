# The Jentosy Project — Domain Context

Glossary only — no implementation details. Updated as terms resolve during wayfinding.

## Terms

**The Jentosy Project, Inc.** — The parent nonprofit organization. Founded by Robert Sanchez, a two-time kidney transplant recipient living with FSGS. The website represents the organization as a whole, not any single initiative. Name origin: "Jentosy" = **JEN** (Keller, Robert's eventual kidney donor) + **...TOSY** (from Elizabeth An**TOSY**, whose entry into a kidney exchange chain made that match possible) — named to honor the two women who saved Robert's life.

**Where We Live** — One initiative under The Jentosy Project, Inc. Does not define or centerpiece the organization's website; the site's scope is the parent org.

**Current mission focus** — Kidney disease education and health equity in underserved communities, with emphasis on rare kidney diseases that disproportionately affect Black and Brown communities: FSGS, APOL1-mediated kidney disease, and IgA Nephropathy. IgA Nephropathy is deliberately included so the organization is not framed as serving only one racial or ethnic population.

**Transplantation equity** — Part of the long-term mission, but presented as a future/secondary area of work on the website, not the current centerpiece. (This is a shift from the organization's original primary focus.)

**Geographic starting point** — The Bronx, with stated intent to expand over time to other underserved communities.

**Founder's story — authoritative account (from the founder directly, supersedes the AI-summarized CBS article numbers below)**:

- 2009: first transplant, a living-donor kidney from a close friend.
- FSGS recurred and attacked that transplanted kidney (medically common with FSGS), leading to dialysis three times weekly by 2014.
- 2019: Robert posted on Facebook seeking a kidney donor. Organ matchmaker **Chaya Lipschutz** found a potential match within about a week: **Elizabeth Antosy**.
- Transplant scheduled for **December 19, 2019**. The night before, transplant surgeon **Dr. Stuart Greenstein** called to cancel — Robert had been found to have kidney cancer and could not be transplanted.
- Elizabeth, heartbroken but undeterred, committed to donating anyway by joining a kidney exchange chain rather than walking away.
- Through that chain, Robert was matched with **Jen Keller**.
- After Robert cleared cancer, he and Jen Keller were transplanted on **October 20, 2021**.
- Elizabeth's decision to join the chain ultimately enabled **8 people** to receive transplants.
- This is the origin of the organization's name (see "The Jentosy Project, Inc." above) and its founding motivation — paying forward what Jen and Elizabeth made possible.

Independently covered by CBS News New York: https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/kidney-transplant-kidney-chain-robert-sanchez-elizabeth-antosy/ — but an AI summarization of that article produced two inaccuracies (transplant month given as "October 2020" and lives-saved given as "4") that do not match the founder's direct account above. Treat the founder's account as authoritative for site copy; re-verify against the article's actual text (not a summary) if exact figures are needed for a public-facing citation.
