## Question

What accessibility standard (e.g. WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA) and specific requirements should the spec commit to, given nonprofit/ADA legal exposure and a healthcare-adjacent, health-equity-focused audience that may include visitors with disabilities or limited digital access?

## Answer

Target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, applied per W3C's full conformance model (not cherry-picked success criteria). DOJ's own Title II rule adopted WCAG 2.1 AA as its binding technical standard, and nonprofits providing health/social services fall within DOJ's "social service center establishment" category of covered public accommodations under Title III — so AA is both the de facto legal bar and the right bar for a health-information/donation site. Full findings, cited to W3C's WCAG 2.1/2.2 Recommendations and DOJ's ADA.gov guidance (including the 2024 Title II final rule), with concrete practices for donation-form accessibility, contrast, screen-reader support for disease-education content, video captioning, and mobile accessibility: [research/accessibility-requirements.md](../research/accessibility-requirements.md).
