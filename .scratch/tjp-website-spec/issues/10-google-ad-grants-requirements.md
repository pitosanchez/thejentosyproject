## Question

What are Google for Nonprofits / Google Ad Grants' eligibility criteria, application process, and ongoing compliance requirements (minimum CTR, campaign/ad-group structure, landing page rules, prohibited practices) as they'd apply to The Jentosy Project, Inc. (an existing 501(c)(3) public charity)?

## Answer

TJP is eligible — none of Google's three excluded org types (government entities, hospitals/healthcare orgs, schools/universities) apply to a standard 501(c)(3). Path: Google for Nonprofits signup via Goodstack verification (2–14 business days, supersedes TechSoup as the current U.S. validator), then Ad Grants activation within that account (welcome video + activation request + review). The $10,000/month cap is a $329/day account cap with a $2 max-CPC ceiling under manual/Maximize Clicks bidding — Smart Bidding (Maximize Conversions/Target CPA/Target ROAS) can exceed the $2 CPC and is mandatory for accounts created after April 22, 2019. Compliance to avoid suspension: ≥5% account-level CTR monthly (two failing months = deactivation), ≥2 ad groups/campaign with ≥2 ads and 2 sitelinks each, mandatory geo-targeting, no single-word/generic or Quality-Score 1–2 keywords, mandatory conversion tracking (≥1/month), Search-only text ads (no Display/video/Shopping). One caveat: the $2-cap/Smart-Bidding detail was confirmed via a search snippet rather than a clean page fetch — spot-check before quoting verbatim in site copy.
