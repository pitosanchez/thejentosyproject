## Question

What belongs in the v1 (MVP) launch scope of The Jentosy Project website, and what is explicitly deferred to a later phase? Produce a clear in/out cutline (pages, features, content depth) that lets the site go live, accept donations, and establish credibility without waiting for everything on the destination's wishlist to be finished.

## Answer

**In v1:** Home; About/Mission (health-equity framing, Bronx-first with stated expansion); Founder's Story; Why This Work Matters (light FSGS/APOL1/IgA Nephropathy education — credibility-level, not a full library); What We Do (programs summary, brief non-centerpiece nod to Where We Live); Partnerships (value prop + inquiry path for researchers/healthcare orgs/funders/policymakers); Donate (Zeffy, one-time + recurring); Leadership/Board & credibility signals; Contact/partnership inquiry form; Privacy Policy/Terms/donor disclosures; SEO/analytics/conversion-tracking foundation built in from day one.

**Deferred:** full patient/community disease-education library; blog/news/press; events calendar; volunteer signup/community-programs portal; newsletter automation beyond basic capture; multi-language support; dedicated transplantation-equity section (future-work mention only); video/testimonial library; dedicated subsites for Where We Live or other initiatives; impact dashboard/detailed metrics; active Google Ad Grants campaign management (tracking hooks ship in v1, campaigns run post-launch).
